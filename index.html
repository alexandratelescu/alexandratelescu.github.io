Physics-informed neural networks for Darcy flow

A custom finite volume solver in OpenFOAM simulates a two-compartment Darcy flow model. Physics-informed neural networks then recover the unknown parameters of that model from the flow data, an inverse problem central to modelling flow in porous media and in tissue.

  • PINNs
  • OpenFOAM
  • DeepXDE
  • PyTorch
